Drive Folder Copier — Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

14-day money-back guarantee

If you're not satisfied with Drive Folder Copier, you can request a full refund within 14 days of your purchase — whether you're on a Monthly, Annual, or Perpetual (one-time) plan. No detailed justification is required.

How to request a refund

Email support@extensionlabs.online with the email address used for your purchase. Refunds are processed by Paddle, our Merchant of Record, and typically appear on your original payment method within 5–10 business days.

After the 14-day window

Purchases are non-refundable after 14 days. For Monthly and Annual subscriptions, you can cancel at any time to stop future billing — your access continues until the end of the current billing period, but no partial refund is issued for the remaining days.

Questions

Contact support@extensionlabs.online with any questions about this policy.
